# ARTIFACTFIRST Source Document Index

## Purpose

This index identifies the source documents required to draft, challenge,
integrate, and retrieve material for the ARTIFACTFIRST master paper. It makes
source authority explicit so that measured case evidence, project decisions,
the Freedom Roads interview packet, and external research do not collapse into
one undifferentiated corpus.

## Retrieval Rules

1. Start with the source class that can actually support the claim.
2. Where sources conflict, use the authority order below rather than averaging
   them into a softer statement.
3. An interview statement describes an operating environment; it does not prove
   an enterprise fact outside that interview context.
4. A resume describes the author's claimed and bounded experience; validate a
   material claim against its underlying project evidence before making it a
   central paper proof point.
5. A project control artifact records a decision or risk. It does not by itself
   prove that a production outcome occurred.
6. External sources support general research claims. They do not prove the
   ARTIFACTFIRST case study.

## Authority Order

| Order | Source class | What it can establish |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Measured case artifact, test, timestamped record, or source-case PDF | Bounded implementation, measured time, documented correction, or source-case event |
| 2 | Canonical master paper and project control records | Current project thesis, decision, risk, evidence boundary, and intended method |
| 3 | Direct interview transcript and raw PD | What Sergey said and what the Freedom Roads role required |
| 4 | Interview-prep and PD-analysis documents | Derived interpretation; use to locate source material, not to override it |
| 5 | Tailored resume | Bounded first-person positioning and claimed experience; validate central proof points elsewhere |
| 6 | External primary research, policy, and provider documentation | General mechanism, law, policy, or current product facts |

## Lookup Playbooks

### To extend a case-study claim

1. Search AF-01 for the actual case event and evidence surface.
2. Check AF-02's proof register and evidence boundary.
3. Record a decision or risk in AF-05 or AF-06 if the claim changes scope,
   framing, or publication exposure.

### To extend the enterprise-governance argument

1. Search FR-01 for the actual role requirement.
2. Search FR-02 for Sergey’s direct operating language.
3. Use FR-04 and FR-05 to locate the honest evidence boundary.
4. Integrate into AF-02 as `interview-derived`, `adjacent evidence`, or
   `proposed enterprise overlay` as appropriate.

### To extend first-person capability language

1. Start with FR-06 for the concise claim and related project family.
2. Validate the central fact against the underlying project artifact before
   making it a paper proof point.
3. Do not convert candidate positioning into a measured enterprise outcome.

### To resolve a model-routing or cost claim

1. Start with AF-09.
2. Follow its official provider links and retrieve the current price or context
   figure.
3. Record the date, product surface, and pricing mode.
4. Treat AF-11 as historical until its conflicting figures are reconciled.
